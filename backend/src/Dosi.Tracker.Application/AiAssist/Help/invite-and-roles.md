---
title: Inviting people and workspace roles
keywords: invite, invitation, member, role, owner, manager, client, permission, team, join
---
A workspace owner or a manager with the right permission can invite someone by email from the Team page. Typing an email that is not yet an active member always creates a pending invitation; the invited person gets a link by email (or you can copy and send the link yourself) and must accept it before they join anything. This is true whether or not they already have an account, so nobody can tell from the response whether an email is registered.

If the invited person is already an active member of the workspace and you are adding them to a specific project, they join that project right away, since they are already trusted members of the workspace.

Workspace roles are owner, manager and member. An owner has full control, including billing and deleting the workspace. A manager can be granted permissions such as viewing the whole team's activity, running reports, or managing team membership — an owner decides which. A plain member normally sees only their own tracked time, unless a manager permission says otherwise.

A project can also have its own roles, including a client role: a client follows a project's progress but is never tracked themselves and never appears in team reports as a tracked worker.

To see or manage pending invitations you sent, check the Team page's invitations list. An invitation that nobody has accepted can be revoked at any time. Someone invited to a workspace must confirm their email before certain self-service AI features become available to them, since those depend on knowing the invitation reached the right address.

Removing a member from the workspace stops their access immediately but keeps their historical tracked time for reporting, unless that data is separately erased under your workspace's data retention settings.
