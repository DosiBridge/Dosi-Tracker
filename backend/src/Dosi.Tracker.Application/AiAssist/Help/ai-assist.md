---
title: What AI assist can and cannot do
keywords: ai, assist, assistant, ask, chatbot, gpt, model, hallucinate, alias
---
AI assist is an optional feature an owner turns on per workspace. Once on, it can write short plain-language summaries of a period, a member's day, or a report, suggest wording for a task note, draft candidate project tasks, and answer questions you type to this assistant — all read-only, and all built strictly from numbers the product already measured.

Before anything reaches the AI provider, every person's name is replaced with an alias such as {ME} for you or a short code for a colleague, and figures are written as data the model must quote rather than recalculate; it is designed so the model cannot invent a number, guess at someone's identity, or make a judgment about a person's performance. If you can see someone's figures elsewhere in the product, AI assist may describe them the same way; if you cannot, it will not either.

The assistant can look up time summaries, daily trends, app usage, attendance, a member's single day (for people you are allowed to see), projects and their tasks, and the product's own help text like this article — nothing else. It cannot create, edit, delete, invite, approve or send anything; it will tell you where in the product to do that instead.

Screenshots, webcam images, keystrokes, raw click and keypress counts, device names, operating system versions and your workspace's name are never sent to the AI provider, whatever else is turned on.

If an answer looks wrong, check the "show the figures used" details under it — every number traces back to a specific measured value — and use the thumbs-up or thumbs-down to tell the workspace owner whether answers are useful. An owner can turn AI assist off entirely at any time from the workspace settings.
